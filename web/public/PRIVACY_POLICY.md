# TranscribeCare Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 8, 2026

## Data Collection

TranscribeCare processes audio recordings locally on your device for speech-to-text transcription. 

### What we collect
- **Audio recordings**: Captured temporarily during active recording sessions. Stored locally on your device only.
- **Transcription text**: Generated from your audio and stored locally in the app's database.
- **Session history**: Stored locally on your device for your convenience.

### What we do NOT collect
- We do not transmit audio or transcription data to external servers.
- We do not collect personal information, analytics, or usage data.
- We do not use advertising SDKs or trackers.

## Permissions

- **Microphone**: Required to record audio for transcription. Only active when you explicitly start a recording.
- **Internet**: Used only if AI-powered features are enabled (optional Gemini API integration).

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using Android's Room database. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data.

## Third-Party Services

If you enable AI summarization features, transcription text may be sent to Google's Gemini API. This is optional and clearly indicated in the app.
